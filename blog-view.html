Open with the problem, in one paragraph. What were you trying to do, and what went wrong or wasn't obvious? A reader should know by the end of this paragraph whether the post is for them.

Body text. Keep paragraphs short. Bold the thing that matters, link out to sources and repos, and use inline code for flags, filenames and function names.

  • A list when the items are genuinely parallel
  • Not when it's really a paragraph in disguise
  • Three to five items reads best
