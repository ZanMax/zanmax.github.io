Support

Help keep the rack running.

The tools, models and write-ups on this site are free, and they stay that way. What isn't free is the hardware they come out of — 32 GPUs that need parts, drives and maintenance. If something here saved you time, this is where you can chip in.

Where it goes

Straight into the cluster.

  • Hardware that wears out Fans, risers, PSUs and drives. Thirty-two cards under sustained load break things on a schedule.
  • Storage for datasets and checkpoints Training runs produce a lot of both, and keeping them around is what makes results reproducible.
  • Time to keep the open-source work going The tooling, the published models, and the posts explaining how any of it works.
  • Power already runs on solar
