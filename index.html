About

From backend systems to GPU racks.

I'm an AI/ML engineer and co-founder of LAB32.AI, where we take AI past the demo stage — fine-tuning and serving models, building RAG and agent systems, and running the infrastructure underneath them so they hold up under real traffic.

A decade of Python, Go, microservices and cloud infrastructure sits underneath all of it. Today that experience goes into private deployments companies can actually own, on 32 RTX 3090s we built and run ourselves.

Most of what I learn ends up as open-source tooling or notes on the blog. Models and datasets land on Hugging Face.
