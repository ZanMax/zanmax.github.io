If you skip the cards

The short version.

  • Running locally, one user GGUF Q4_K_M. It runs on anything and the quality is excellent for the size. Step to Q5/Q6 if VRAM allows.
  • Serving many users on a GPU AWQ or FP8 on vLLM/SGLang. FP8 if you're on Ada/Hopper/Blackwell and want near-lossless; AWQ for 4-bit at scale.
  • Max quality on one consumer card EXL2/EXL3 — dial the exact bits-per-weight to fill your 24 GB and no more.
  • Fine-tuning a big model on little VRAM NF4, via QLoRA. It's a training base, not a serving format — merge and re-quantize afterwards.
