Weights and KV cache are computed exactly from the model config; the activation slice is a coarse estimate that varies with framework and attention kernel. Real usage runs a little higher than the sum — leave headroom. QLoRA forces the frozen base to 4-bit regardless of the precision picker.

The math

Nothing up the sleeve.

  • Weights params × bytes-per-weight. FP16 is 2 bytes, INT8/FP8 is 1, INT4/NF4 is 0.5. A 70B model at 4-bit is ~35 GB before anything else.
  • KV cache — the long-context killer 2 × layers × kv-heads × head-dim × context × batch × 2 bytes. Grows linearly with context, and at 128k it can dwarf the weights. GQA (few KV heads) is what keeps it survivable.
  • Optimizer state (training only) Full fine-tuning with Adam needs ~16 bytes/param — weights, gradients, fp32 master copy and two Adam moments. It's why full FT wants many times the inference memory. LoRA and QLoRA only pay this on the tiny adapters.
  • How many GPUs Total ÷ (VRAM × 0.92), rounded up. The 0.92 is because CUDA context, fragmentation and the framework never leave you the whole card.
